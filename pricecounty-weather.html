Price County Weather

A free Chrome extension for Wisconsin's Northwoods

Price County Weather brings real-time weather for Price County, Wisconsin directly to your Chrome toolbar. The extension badge shows the current temperature at a glance — no need to open a weather app or website.

Features

  • Temperature badge — Current temp displayed in the extension icon, updated every 30 minutes
  • Full conditions — Click the icon for current weather, feels-like temperature, wind speed/direction, humidity, visibility, and barometric pressure
  • 24-hour forecast — Hourly forecast for the next 24 hours with icons and descriptions
  • Severe weather alerts — Active NWS alerts for Price County (Zone WIZ011) shown in the popup
  • 5 locations — Switch between Park Falls, Phillips, Fifield, Prentice, and Catawba
  • Units toggle — Fahrenheit or Celsius

Data Sources

  • Current conditions and forecast: OpenWeatherMap
  • Severe weather alerts: National Weather Service (NWS)

Install

Install Price County Weather from the Chrome Web Store. Search for "Price County Weather" or visit our listing page.

Privacy

This extension does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data. It fetches weather data from public APIs (OpenWeatherMap and NWS) using geographic coordinates for Price County cities. Your location preference and unit settings are stored locally on your device. See our privacy policy.
